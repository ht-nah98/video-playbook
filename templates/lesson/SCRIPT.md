# __TITLE__

Narration script (English).

**Format (read by `tools/voice/narrate.py`):**
- one line = one breath = one synthesis take (a sentence, or two tightly linked ones); 0.35 s pause after it
- blank line = end of an idea; 0.95 s pause
- `##` = a stop (1.6 s), `# ACT` = act card (2.4 s), `[INTRO]` = the channel signature
- prose with soul, not beat-sized fragments; numbers and symbols written as WORDS — and each one gets a row in
  `spoken.json` (spoken → written) before publish; an acronym read as a word (SOH-CAH-TOA) is written lower-case with
  spaces ("soh cah toa") + one spoken.json row (LESSONS §128)
- length: narration words ÷ 190 = minutes (Atlas voice), not the source script's timestamps (LESSONS §129)

**Shape (docs/lesson/LESSON-VIDEO.md, "Học từ video mẫu số Pi"):**
- the promise names two or three questions; they go on screen as a numbered list, and each act answers one
- a misconception is said, then corrected ("You might think… It's actually…"); the picture strikes the wrong one
- an intuition that is not a proof says so out loud
- "Putting it together" gives each answer in one sentence, over the picture that first explained it
- the last idea is something the viewer can notice on their own, before the thanks and the channel board

## Cold open

Hook line — the promise itself; names the thumbnail's object within ~3 s, no lead-in.
What this video is, and why it matters to you.
The two or three questions this video answers.
A hanging line that leads straight into Act One (the English channel has no channel intro).

[INTRO]

# ACT ONE — …

## …

# ACT … — Putting it together

## Putting it together

One sentence per answer, in the order of the promise — short; the high point is the answer just before it.

## Next time

Something the viewer can notice or do on their own.
Thanks and subscribe (channel board "99% from scratch" stays at least 4 s).
